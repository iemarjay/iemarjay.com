### Common Questions & How to Answer

#### "Why do you want to work here?"

**Strategy**: Show you researched them + connect your experience to their needs.

**Template:**
> "I've been [following your company / working in this space] for [time]. What stands out is [specific thing about their product/culture]. I have [relevant experience] that directly applies to [their problem]. Also, [personal connection if any]."

**Example (DeFi company):**
> "I've been working in DeFi for 2 years at Pouchfi, building deposit/withdrawal systems. What stands out about [Company] is your focus on [specific thing]. I recently audited a prediction market protocol and found critical bugs — so I understand the security tradeoffs in DeFi. Plus, I'm building my own product on the side, so I understand the pressure to ship while maintaining quality."

#### "What's your experience with [specific technology]?"

**Strategy**: Be honest. If you have experience, quantify it. If not, show learning ability.

**If you have experience:**
> "I've used [tech] for [X years/months] on [project]. Built [specific thing]. Most recently [recent example]."

**If you're learning/adjacent:**
> "I haven't used [tech] in production yet, but I've used [similar tech] extensively. I pick up new frameworks quickly — for example, I learned [example] in [timeframe]. Would take me [realistic estimate] to be productive."

**Example (if asked about Go):**
> "I haven't used Go in production, but I'm proficient in TypeScript/Node and have read Go codebases. Compiled languages, strong typing, and concurrency patterns are familiar. Would take me 2-3 weeks to be productive for most tasks."

#### "What's your salary expectation?"

**Strategy**: Anchor high but show flexibility.

**Template:**
> "I'm looking for $[target]/mo ($[annual]/year), but I'm flexible based on the full package (equity, benefits, growth opportunity). What's the range for this role?"

## Red Flags to Avoid

### In Applications

**Don't apply if:**
- Salary is below $2,500/mo with no equity
- Job description says "rockstar" or "ninja" (usually bad culture)
- Company asks for free work beyond 4 hours
- "Urgent hiring" + "wear many hats" + low pay (burnout risk)
- Anonymous job posts with no company name
- Requires relocation with no remote option

### In Job Descriptions

**Watch out for:**
- "Fast-paced" + "startup" + "equity heavy, low base" = risk
- "Must be available 24/7" = no boundaries
- No salary range provided = probably low or waste of time
- "We're like a family" = red flag for boundaries

### In Interview Process

**Red flags:**
- More than 5 interview rounds (respect your time)
- Take-home project longer than 4 hours (ask for compensation)
- Disrespectful communication or ghosting
- Pressuring you to decide immediately

## Decision-Making Framework for AI Assistant

### Should I Apply? (Yes/No Checklist)

**Apply if ALL of these are true:**
- [ ] Role is Senior-level or above
- [ ] Salary is $3,000+/mo or equity compensates
- [ ] Remote or Nigeria-friendly
- [ ] Job description matches 60%+ of skills
- [ ] Company/role is legitimate (not scam)
- [ ] Application can be tailored (enough info to customize)

**Don't apply if ANY of these are true:**
- [ ] Salary is below $2,500/mo with no equity
- [ ] Requires relocation with no remote option
- [ ] Junior/Mid-level role only
- [ ] Pure front-end or pure DevOps
- [ ] Red flags in job description (see above)
- [ ] Not enough information to tailor application

### How to Tailor Application

**Step 1: Identify role type**
- Backend-heavy? Full-stack? Web3? Fintech? Security?

**Step 2: Match experience to role**
- What's the strongest relevant experience?
- Which case study or story fits best?

**Step 3: Research company**
- What are they building?
- Any recent news, blog posts, or tweets?
- Can you reference something specific?

**Step 4: Write opening hook**
- Use template from relevant example
- Inject specific research from Step 3

**Step 5: Choose 2-3 key experiences**
- Match to their needs (from job description)
- Use specific numbers/details

**Step 6: Add unique angle**
- $10k story if fintech/payment
- Audit story if security/Web3
- Product story if product company
- Team leadership if they emphasize that

**Step 7: Close with links**
- Always include portfolio: iemarjay.com
- Add relevant case study link
- GitHub if they care about open source

### Tone Guidelines

**Voice**: Practical, friendly, confident but humble

**Good:**
- "I've built X for Y years"
- "Found 17 issues including [specific]"
- "Learned the hard way" (about $10k)
- "Would love to chat"

**Avoid:**
- "I'm the best at X"
- "I'm passionate about blockchain" (too generic)
- "Dear hiring manager" (too formal)
- Long paragraphs (keep it scannable)

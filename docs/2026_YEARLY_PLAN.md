## Overview

This plan maps out monthly priorities across:
- **Projects**: Shuttershow, client work, audit contests
- **Content**: Blog, Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube (6 videos/year), **Shorts/Micro Vlogs** (2-3/week across X, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram)
- **Career**: Job search → job stability → growth
- **Travel**: Strategic conferences and networking events

**Time budget**: 10-15 hours/week brand building + 5-8 hours/week audit study

4. **Flexibility** — Adjust quarterly based on results
5. **Travel strategically** — 1-2 high-impact conferences, not conference hopping

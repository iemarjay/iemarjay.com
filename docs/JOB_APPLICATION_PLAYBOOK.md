# Job Application Playbook

**Purpose**: Tactical guide for applying to jobs. For brand positioning, experience details, and goals, see [PERSONAL_BRAND_STRATEGY.md](./PERSONAL_BRAND_STRATEGY.md).

---

## Should I Apply? (Checklist)

**Apply if ALL true:**
- Role is Senior-level or above
- Salary is $3,000+/mo or equity compensates
- Remote or Nigeria-friendly
- Job description matches 60%+ of skills
- Company/role is legitimate
- Enough info to tailor the application

**Don't apply if ANY true:**
- Salary below $2,500/mo with no equity
- Requires relocation with no remote option
- Junior/Mid-level only
- Pure front-end or pure DevOps
- Red flags (see below)

---

## Red Flags

**In job descriptions:**
- "Rockstar" or "ninja" (bad culture)
- "Must be available 24/7" (no boundaries)
- "We're like a family" (boundary issues)
- "Fast-paced startup" + "equity heavy, low base" (risk)
- No salary range = probably low
- Free work beyond 4 hours

**In interview process:**
- More than 5 rounds
- Take-home longer than 4 hours (ask for compensation)
- Pressuring immediate decisions

## Cover Letter Structure (150-250 words max)

```
[Personal greeting or "Hey,"]
[Hook — 1-2 sentences showing you researched them]
[Most relevant experience — 2-3 sentences matching their needs]
[Unique angle — 1-2 sentences differentiating you]
[Low-pressure CTA]
[Links: iemarjay.com + relevant case study]
Emmanuel
```

**Hook examples:**
- "I saw your post about building [specific thing] and had to reach out."
- "I read your blog post on [topic] — I've dealt with that exact issue."

**Unique angles by role type:**
- Fintech/payments → $10k webhook story → $5,110 forensic recovery
- Prediction markets → Solana audit (17 issues, vault insolvency in 2,800 lines of Rust)
- Security roles → Cross-layer replay attack pattern (web2 webhook → web3 signature)
- AI / dev tools → Code Refinery (open-source agentic PR review on Claude Code CLI)
- CTO / Engineering Lead → Pouchfi (built platform + hired all 7 engineers), $400K raise at Origneum
- Product companies → Building Shuttershow

**CTA — good:** "Would love to chat about what you're building."
**CTA — avoid:** "I'm the perfect fit!" / "Looking forward to hearing from you"

## Platform-Specific Tips

- "Open to Work" set to private (recruiters only)

## What to Emphasize vs De-emphasize

**Always emphasize:** 8 years production experience, Wikimedia Foundation, real money systems, security mindset, team leadership, Shuttershow, backend depth, case studies with links, web2+web3 combo

**Role-dependent:** Smart contract audits (Web3 only), LMSR/prediction markets (prediction market companies only), Laravel (if they use it)

**Never mention:** Current salary ($1,500/mo), desperation, negative experiences, politics

## Application Workflow

1. Identify role type (backend/full-stack/web3/fintech/security)
2. Match strongest experience to their needs
3. Research company (10 min — website, blog, Twitter)
4. Write hook referencing specific research
5. Choose 2-3 key experiences with numbers
6. Add unique angle ($10k/audit/product/leadership)
7. Close with portfolio link + relevant case study

**Time per application:** ~30-35 minutes
**Weekly target:** 5-10 quality applications

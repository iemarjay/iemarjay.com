---

## Script

```
[0-5s] HOOK
"A webhook bug cost my client $10,000. Here's what I learned."

[5-20s] SETUP
"I built a payment system. Thought it was solid.
Then someone discovered you could replay webhook requests...
and they did. $10,000 gone."

[20-35s] IMPACT
"My client lost money. I lost their trust.
And I couldn't sleep for weeks.
That moment broke something in me — in a good way."

[35-55s] TRANSFORMATION
"I became obsessed with security.
Studied every vulnerability class.
Built audit methodologies.
Learned to think like an attacker."

[55-75s] REDEMPTION
"Months later, they called me back. This time to audit their systems.
I found 24 vulnerabilities — including the exact type that hit us before.
Did forensic analysis. Tracked $7,000 in fraud. Found $5,000 still recoverable."

[75-90s] LESSON
"The lesson? Your worst failure can become your greatest expertise.
Now I find the bugs before the hackers do."
```

---

## Visual Notes

- Serious tone for hook and setup
- Show vulnerability (not afraid to admit mistake)
- Build intensity during transformation section
- Triumphant energy for redemption
- End with confident, warm energy
